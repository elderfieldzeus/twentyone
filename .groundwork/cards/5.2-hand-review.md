## Goal
Let users review each saved decision in a completed hand.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A user can open a saved hand from local history.
- [ ] Previous and next controls move through every player decision.
- [ ] Each step restores the cards, selected move, probabilities, grade, and rules.
- [ ] Review navigation cannot change the original saved record.
- [ ] Keyboard, touch, and screen-reader users can operate review controls.
