## Goal
Model cards, decks, shuffling, dealing, and shoe state independently from the interface.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A deck contains the correct ranks, suits, and blackjack values.
- [ ] A shoe supports the configured deck count and deals each card at most once.
- [ ] A shuffle can start a fresh shoe after each hand.
- [ ] A persistent shoe reports the exact cards that remain across hands.
- [ ] Automated tests cover one-deck and six-deck shoes.
