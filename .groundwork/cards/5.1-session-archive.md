## Goal
Save complete sessions and hands locally for later learning review.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Archived sessions include their summary, rules, hands, and time.
- [ ] Saved hands include cards, actions, grades, probabilities, and result.
- [ ] Archives survive a page reload without a server or database.
- [ ] Users can delete saved history through a separate confirmed action.
- [ ] Invalid saved data does not prevent the app from starting.
