## Goal
Give immediate move feedback without revealing the next decision early.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The panel stays hidden until the player makes a move.
- [ ] It shows every move available before that action with probabilities and expected value.
- [ ] It labels the selected move with text that does not depend on color.
- [ ] After a hit, feedback remains visible while the next best move stays hidden.
- [ ] Split hands receive feedback for the correct decision and hand.
