## Goal
Give immediate move feedback without revealing the next decision early.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] The panel stays hidden until the player makes a move.
- [x] It shows every move available before that action with probabilities and expected value.
- [x] It labels the selected move with text that does not depend on color.
- [x] After a hit, feedback remains visible while the next best move stays hidden.
- [x] Split hands receive feedback for the correct decision and hand.

## How to check
- Run `npx playwright test tests/e2e/move-analysis.spec.ts`.
- Open the app at desktop and phone widths.
- Deal a pair, then select Split and Stand in separate hands.
- Confirm each panel appears within 15 seconds.
- Confirm only the Split row says `Estimated` and `Representative shoe sample`.
