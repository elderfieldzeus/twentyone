## Goal
Score all player and dealer hands under the configured blackjack rules.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Hand scoring handles hard totals, soft totals, multiple aces, 21, and busts.
- [ ] A two-card 21 is blackjack unless it follows a split.
- [ ] Equal totals and equal blackjacks produce a push.
- [ ] Blackjack payout supports 3:2 and 6:5 rules.
- [ ] Automated tests cover each scoring edge case.
